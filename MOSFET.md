# MOSFET 

**MOSFET stands for Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistor.**  

***A MOSFET is a voltage-controlled semiconductor device in which the gate voltage creates an electric field across an insulating oxide layer, forming or modifying a channel that controls current flow between the source and drain.***  
In simple words, a MOSFET acts like an electrically controlled switch. A small voltage applied at its Gate can control whether current flows through its Drain and Source, and how much current flows.  

Unlike a conventional switch, it doesn't need a mechanical movement. It controls current electronically.  

## Why MOSFET Developed

Electronic circuits need a way to control current, switch devices ON and OFF, and amplify electrical signals.
For example, imagine a circuit in which a relatively small control signal(small control voltage) needs to control a larger current. A MOSFET allows this by using the voltage at its Gate to control the conductivity of the channel between Drain and Source.  

Its main idea is:
**A voltage controls the conductivity of a semiconductor channel, which controls the current.**

**Gate(G)** - **Controls the conductivity of the channel**  

**Drain(D)** - **Terminal through which conventional current enters the channel in typical n-channel operation**  

**Source(S)** - **Terminal from which electrons enter the channel in typical n-channel operation**  

**Substrate(Body)** - **Semiconductor base on which the device is constructed**  

***The Gate is separated from the channel region by a thin layer of silicon dioxide (SiO₂), which acts as an insulator.***  

## How does a MOSFET work

The MOSFET works through the effect of an electric field.  
When a voltage is applied to the Gate, it creates an electric field in the semiconductor region beneath it. Depending on the MOSFET type and the applied voltage, this electric field can:  

•Create a conducting channel  
•Increase the conductivity of an existing channel  
•Decrease the conductivity of an existing channel  

## Types of MOSFET

There are two basic types of MOSFET: 

**• Enhancement MOSFET (E-MOSFET)**  
**• Depletion MOSFET (D-MOSFET)**  

## Why engineers prefer MOSFETs

**High input impedance:** Because the Gate is insulated, it draws very little current under normal operation.  

**Low power consumption:** Very little steady-state power is required at the Gate in many applications.  

**Fast switching:** It can switch between ON and OFF states very quickly.  

**Small size:** It can be manufactured at microscopic scales.  

**Efficient control:** It can control current using a relatively small control voltage.
